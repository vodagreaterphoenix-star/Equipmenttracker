Help & Guide
How to use the Equipment Tracker
1Getting started
The app is a bookmarked web link — nothing to install. The first time, tap Share (iPhone) or the ⋮ menu (Android), then "Add to Home Screen," so it opens like a normal app icon from then on.
If the app doesn't load, check your internet connection first — it needs a live connection to sync with the shop's records.

If a job's equipment comes back in batches, that's fine — just repeat this each time. The job stays "Active" until everything on it has been returned.
4Equipment in Maintenance
Equipment can go to Maintenance two ways: automatically, if it's flagged "Needs Repair" or "Out of Service" at check-in from a job — or directly from the shop, if something's found damaged before it ever goes out. Open the item and tap "Send to maintenance" to do that.
Either way, it cannot be checked out directly to a job while in Maintenance. Once it's actually fixed, open the item and tap "Mark fixed — return to shop." Only then can it be checked out again.
5Moving equipment & locations
Use "Move equipment" on Home to relocate one or several items at once — for example, loading a van before heading to a job site, or moving gear between shop areas.
"Manage locations" lets you set up the vans, trailers, or shop areas your team actually uses, so they show up when moving equipment.
6Scanning & finding equipment
Tap "Scan" on Home to look up a specific item fast — point the camera at its tag.
Use the Inventory tab to search by name, type, or model, or filter by status (Shop, On a Job, Missing, Maintenance).
7Job history & reports
Open any job to see a two-column report — everything Sent Out, and everything Returned to Shop, each with its date.
Reports can be printed or saved as a PDF directly from the job page.
8Adding, editing & removing equipment
Add Equipment (Home) — enter the type, model, and an ID tag. The ID tag is what gets scanned, so make sure it's applied to the item itself.
Edit — open any item's page to correct its details.
Recently Deleted (Home) — accidentally removed something? Restore it here.
9Managing the team
Manage Team (Home) — keep this current; it's what populates "assigned to" during checkout.
10Key terms
Job — a customer and address that equipment is checked out to.
Active Job — currently has equipment checked out to it.
Post Job — everything on it has been returned to the shop.
Questions this guide doesn't answer? Ask your manager — this covers the day-to-day basics, not every edge case.
